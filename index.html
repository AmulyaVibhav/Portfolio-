Telco Customer Churn Analysis and Prediction

I explored 7,043 customer records to see who is likely to leave and why, then trained Logistic Regression and Random Forest models. Logistic Regression did better on recall, which matters most when the goal is to catch customers before they go. The results sit in a six-page Streamlit dashboard with KPIs, segments, trends, model evaluation, feature importance and a churn-risk score.

Built with: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Streamlit
